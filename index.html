Notify When Gatita Is Done?

                
            

            

                
Gatita can send a browser notification when a response finishes, so you can switch tabs and come back when the answer is ready.

                

                    Optional browser permission
                    Notifications are only used for finished Ask responses. You can turn them off again in the account menu or your browser settings.
                

                

                    
                    
                

            

        

    


    

        

            

                
Before You Use Gatita Ask

            

            

                
Gatita Ask requires acceptance of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before the chat website can be used in this browser.

                

                    Required cookies and storage
                    Cookies, local storage, Turnstile verification, session tokens, and security checks are required for login, spam prevention, rate limiting, API protection, saved chats, and abuse prevention. They cannot be disabled while using Gatita Ask.
                

                
                
            

        

    


    

        

            Required cookies
            
Gatita Ask uses required cookies and local storage for security, sessions, spam prevention, and saved preferences. There are no optional cookie categories to disable.
