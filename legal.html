Privacy Policy

1. Privacy Commitment

Gatita Ask treats chat privacy as a priority. Chats are private by default, are not sold, and are not used for training or service enhancement. Gatita aims to be privacy-conscious and eco-friendly by limiting energy use, avoiding all possible tracking, and only using energy from renewable sources.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, password hash and salt, account creation/update time, and last login time.
  • Session and security data: hashed session tokens, session expiration, user-agent text, hashed IP-related data, guest identifier, browser security challenge results, and request metadata needed to prevent spam and abuse.
  • Chat data: chat titles, messages, model/personality settings, thinking/research settings, sources, shared-link tokens, pinned/folder settings, and timestamps.
  • File data: file name, content type, size, and submitted content when needed to answer your request. The service stores file metadata with the chat; readable file content is used for the AI request.
  • Usage data: per-minute message events and Deep research usage events so limits can be enforced.
  • Browser storage: local browser storage may keep your session token, guest ID, selected model, personality, thinking/research settings, and interface preferences.

3. Why We Collect It

  • To create accounts, authenticate users, and keep users signed in.
  • To save chats, messages, shared links, pins, folders, and per-chat preferences.
  • To process prompts, files, images, and research requests.
  • To verify browser requests and reduce spam, bots, scraping, abuse, and automated attacks.
  • To enforce message-per-minute limits and Deep research limits.
  • To debug reliability and protect the service, users, and backend API.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Gatita Ask uses local storage and may use cookies or similar browser technologies for security, authentication, verification, preferences, and spam reduction. Browser security checks may set, read, or compute challenge tokens to confirm that requests are legitimate. These technologies are used to protect the service, not for ad targeting.

5. AI Processing

Your messages, relevant chat context, uploaded content, selected settings, and current session details are sent to our servers to generate responses. Gatita does not use this data for training, fine-tuning, analytics datasets, advertising, profiling, resale, or service enhancement.

6. Research and Auto Web Check

If you enable Research, Deep research, or Auto web check, Gatita Ask may generate search queries, send those queries to a search provider, fetch result pages, and include source snippets in the AI context. Source websites may receive server-side requests from Gatita Ask. Links used by the AI may be shown under Sources.

7. Sharing

Chats are private unless you create a share link. A shared chat can be viewed by anyone who has the link. Shared links use random tokens and can be disabled by deleting the chat or disabling sharing where available.

8. Data Retention

Chats and account data are retained so the service can provide chat history and account access. Deleted chats are archived or removed from normal access. Some security and usage records may remain for a limited time to prevent abuse, investigate incidents, meet legal obligations, or maintain service integrity.

9. Data We Do Not Sell or Train On

Gatita does not sell your personal information. Gatita does not use your chats, files, prompts, responses, account information, or usage records to train AI models or enhance services. No chat content is used to build public or private training datasets.

10. Security

Gatita uses privacy-minded security practices such as password hashing, session token hashing, origin checks, browser security challenges, rate limiting, and limited data collection. No internet service can guarantee perfect security, but Gatita designs Ask to collect only what is needed and to protect stored data with care.

11. Children

Gatita Ask is not intended for children under 13. Do not use the service if you are under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact Gatita so the information can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.
